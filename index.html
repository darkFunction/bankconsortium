Recommended design

Private Solidity for interbank payments at retail volume

Keep this PoC's programming model: Solidity running privately among the banks involved, plus zero-knowledge cash. Change the shared ledger so it checks proofs on every core before ordering payments, under a fast DAG-based consensus. Each payment settles individually and is final in about a second, with no settlement batching and no L2. The page walks through one payment end to end, explains how the consensus meets Solidity, compares Polymesh and Hedera, and sets out the build step by step.

Alternatives

Rayls & Nightfall

The two products closest to “Solidity plus a shielded pool for banks”, examined through their open-source code.

  • Rayls: ~1–4 payments/s across 20 banks without batching, with 20–60 s latency, and the operator can decrypt everything.
  • Nightfall_4: ~6k gas per transfer, but one huge rollup prover, no programmability, and still experimental.

The short answer

Is this ready for a national network?

  • Besu: yes for wholesale RTGS volumes (a few to ~10 payments/s); no for retail instant payments on one chain.
  • Paladin: not yet. Wholesale volume is within reach, but v0.15 failed 3.5% of payments, and it can generate only one Zeto proof at a time per bank per token.
  • Retail volume with privacy and Solidity: no product does it today, Polymesh included, but there is a viable design built from parts already in production.
